This direction supersedes the repeated factory scenes described in the earlier factory-story record. Shared materials and lighting provide continuity; scene geometry and visual actions now differ by concept.

## Four authored experiences

- **Owned facility:** the existing supported production journey gains an exterior roof/cutaway reveal, rooflights, HVAC, glazed annex, stairs, dock frames, service racks and reserved material aisles. Inspection gets a bounded camera emphasis. The part, truck and connector timing from the previous refinement remain.
- **Partnership development:** a separate glass planning surface, architectural grid and access road establish the site. Structural columns rise; pitched roof bays arrive; three machine installations and utility/control connections resolve. A roof offset exposes the proposed installation. This uses its own geometry, rather than another scaled instance of the operating factory.
- **Shared capability:** different treatment, CNC inspection and assembly cells surround an elevated quality-workflow screen. Identity, requirement, observation, inspection and disposition nodes connect with orthogonal routes; the lower workflow nodes rearrange while the reusable method is packaged. Each cell demonstrates a different local application. No coating recipe is represented as universally transferable.
- **Engineering development:** exact Manufacturing hero bracket profiles are reused in a new assembly study with base, upright, bore collar and two axial fasteners. Parts separate along their locating axes, pause and recombine. The assembled bracket seats on a glass engineering bench. Desktop includes a brief diagram and line study; phone prioritises the component close-up. This remains explicitly a future concept study.

## Implementation

`authored-scenes.ts` owns the three new compositions and the operating-facility enrichment. `authored-renderer.ts` supplies their shared studio, deterministic surface sampling, finite visible-time playback, adaptive DPR and suspended offscreen rendering. `bracket-study.ts` retains the exact approved hero profiles and bore geometry. The existing network renderer dispatches each concept to the correct renderer.

Phone capability chapters move between the local cell and shared screen; the packaging view has closer framing and larger labels. Partnership framing includes the complete roof envelope. Native renderer captures replace all sixteen previous desktop/mobile/theme fallbacks. No dependency, external model, route or navigation change was added.
