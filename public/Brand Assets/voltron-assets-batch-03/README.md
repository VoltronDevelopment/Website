# Voltron assets — Batch 03

Five families, each in light and dark: Plant Configuration, Voltron AI, system responsibilities, ontology identity relationships and manufacturing relationships.

## Deliverables
10 grouped SVGs; 20 transparent PNGs at 1×/2×; preview; asset manifest with live-label positions; theme-switching animation demonstration. Same palette, geometry and strokes as Batches 01 and 02.

Display sizes: configuration and AI 240 × 240; responsibilities 900 × 240; ontology diagrams 440 × 300. The responsibility family contains three independently grouped symbols in one composition. SVGs remain resolution independent. Labels are not baked into production geometry.

ERP owns business and production records. QMS owns quality records and decisions. PLC retains physical control. Shared-context relationships are not control commands. The identity diagram has Lot at its centre, related to Plant, Part, Operation and Machine. The manufacturing diagram connects context with Requirements, Processes, Equipment, Inspection and Evidence. These are schematic illustrations, not runtime fact graphs.

Module, connection and detail groups have stable IDs for animation. The demo illustrates reveal behavior and includes reduced-motion fallback. Final page interactions, responsive layout and accessible live labels must be integrated and verified.
