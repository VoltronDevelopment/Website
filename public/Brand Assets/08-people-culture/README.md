# People Culture assets

Route: `/about/people-culture`

The animated hero reuses ../voltron people culture/ hand artwork and its foreground mask; keep those files in place.

- `P03-individual-whole-light.webp` and `P03-individual-whole-dark.webp`: Individual within a larger whole. Recommended source: 1200 x 900px.
- `P04-responsibility-light.webp` and `P04-responsibility-dark.webp`: Growth through responsibility. Recommended source: 1200 x 900px.
- `P05-perspectives-light.webp` and `P05-perspectives-dark.webp`: Distinct perspectives. Recommended source: 600 x 600px.
- `P05-shared-purpose-light.webp` and `P05-shared-purpose-dark.webp`: Shared purpose. Recommended source: 600 x 600px.
- `P05-mutual-growth-light.webp` and `P05-mutual-growth-dark.webp`: Mutual growth. Recommended source: 600 x 600px.

Transparent WebP is preferred. A matching `-light.svg` or `-dark.svg` takes precedence when supplied. For PNG, change the corresponding manifest `image` filename to `.png`. Upload only one format per theme. Keep the same composition, crop and aspect ratio in both themes. Do not include UI text, labels, buttons or a baked page background. Existing source assets must remain intact. Missing files reserve the artwork space without broken-image icons.
