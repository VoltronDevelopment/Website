## Source priority
A current approved public claim with evidence takes precedence over drafts. Next use explicit owner-confirmed public decisions. Use dated drafts for intended direction, preserving their qualification. Prior assistant suggestions and recalled summaries must not establish operational fact. Newer wording does not automatically make a claim verified.

## Excluded material
Proprietary architecture documents, implementation internals, private personnel discussions, customer records, credentials and sensitive operating details are not included. Original source documents are not bundled because they contain editorial and private material beyond the public-safe corpus.
