# Industrial product development

## Short answer
Voltron intends to use recurring needs encountered in industrial work as a starting point for selected proprietary product development. This is a future direction alongside manufacturing expansion.

## Distinct growth paths
Manufacturing expansion extends process capability and operating environments. Product development turns selected problems into engineered solutions that need their own discovery, validation and support. These paths can inform one another without being the same business activity.

## Timing and ambition
The ten-year vision places the beginning of dedicated product development around Year 5, subject to validation and priorities. The ambition is to compete globally on innovation, technology and quality with Indian roots. No current catalog, orderable product, release date or global certification follows from that ambition.

## Visitor response
For a proposed product collaboration, understand the problem, application and desired outcome before discussing fit. Do not promise customization or development commitments on behalf of the company.
