# Voltron Alpha and surface treatment

## Short answer
Voltron Alpha is the first manufacturing environment in Voltron’s plan for developing and validating its approach under real production conditions, initially focused on surface treatment.

## Readiness
Alpha is in development. Commissioning, commercial readiness, physical validation and CED certification remain unconfirmed. Confirm availability and suitability for the specific requirement with the production team.

## Process scope discussed
Phosphating, CED and powder coating appear in the public content direction. Their individual operating status and suitability must be confirmed. Do not infer capacity, substrate compatibility, corrosion performance, test hours, certification or delivery dates from illustrative artwork.

## Why this application
Surface treatment brings preparation, processing, equipment, quality evidence and release into a connected sequence. What happens at one stage can affect another. This makes it a useful initial setting for examining coherent production and learning from practical conditions.

## Intended production conversation
Understand the part and requirements; review the proposed process route; establish applicable inspection needs; confirm scope and readiness; then agree the next commercial or technical step. A chat is not a quotation or production booking.

## Information a visitor may share
Part/application, required process or need for guidance, expected volume and units, quality specifications and timeline. Do not request confidential drawings or process recipes in ordinary chat. Use an approved secure channel if technical files are needed.

## Illustrations
Three-tank demonstrations, thirteen-tank line artwork, gantries and animated components in earlier design discussions are marketing representations. They must not become factual plant specifications in answers.
