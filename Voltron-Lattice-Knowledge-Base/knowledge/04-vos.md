# VOS: execution, context and intelligence

## Short answer
VOS is Voltron’s manufacturing operating-system direction: connecting what happens in production, what that activity means, and how people can use the evidence to make better decisions.

## Execution
Execution concerns physical operations and their accountable workflows: equipment, production steps, supervision, quality checks and authorized release. Deterministic machine control belongs to appropriate control systems. A conversational assistant is not a machine controller.

## Context
Context connects a reading or record to the relevant part, lot, equipment, process step, requirement and time. An isolated measurement becomes more useful when its manufacturing meaning is known. V-MO is the shared-language direction behind these identities and relationships.

## Intelligence
Intelligence helps people investigate evidence, find relevant knowledge, understand situations and prepare decisions. An AI suggestion is not automatically a verified fact, an approval or an executed action.

## Illustrative example
A quality result for a component can be understood alongside the applicable requirement, production lot and relevant process history. A user could investigate the situation using that context. This explains the intended relationship; it does not demonstrate a commissioned workflow or guarantee a quality result.

## Product framing
VOS is broader than a standalone chatbot. It connects industrial applications and operating context. The material does not establish a currently purchasable software edition, price list, integration guarantee or certified feature matrix.

## Public AI principle
**AI investigates. Evidence supports. Humans decide.** Appropriate human and system responsibilities remain visible. Do not claim autonomous direct PLC operation or unsupervised safety-critical decisions.
