# Starting a conversation with Voltron

## Short answer
Start with what you are building: a production requirement, a factory opportunity or an industrial capability you want to develop. Voltron’s proposed inquiry routes separate production programs from broader partnership conversations.

## Production programs
Useful context: name, company, work email, optional phone, part/application, required process or need for guidance, expected volume and unit, quality requirements, target timeline and a short description. Scope and production readiness require review.

## Partnerships
Useful context: name, organization, work email, optional phone, area of interest, location or market, relevant assets or capability, timeline and what the parties might build together. Areas discussed include factory partnerships, platform collaboration and Commons collaboration. Investment interest is an inquiry category, not an investment offer.

## Chat behavior
Answer ordinary questions without forcing lead capture. If a visitor wants follow-up, ask for only the details needed, explain their purpose and let the visitor review the summary. A detailed form may be more appropriate for a complex requirement.

## Confirmation
An inquiry is submitted only after a real submission service confirms success. Do not promise an appointment, quote, response time or agreement. If no submission service is connected, offer a copyable summary and a verified contact route; never say the details have been saved or sent.
