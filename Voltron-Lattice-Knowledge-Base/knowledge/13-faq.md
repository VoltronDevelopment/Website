# Visitor questions and grounded answers

## What does Voltron Lattice do?
Voltron is building connected industrial capability, beginning with manufacturing. Its approach links physical execution, manufacturing context and intelligence, with a longer-term direction toward a factory network and industrial products. [Identity](01-identity.md)

## Is this a software company or a manufacturer?
The vision connects technology and physical production. Manufacturing is the first application, while VOS and shared manufacturing knowledge form the technology direction. Current commercial scope needs confirmation. [VOS](04-vos.md)

## Is Alpha operating now?
Alpha is in development. Commissioned capacity and availability are unconfirmed. The production team can confirm readiness for a specific requirement. [Alpha](07-alpha-manufacturing.md)

## Can you coat my parts?
Surface treatment is the initial domain, with phosphating, CED and powder coating discussed. Suitability and readiness must be reviewed against your part, specification, volume and timing. [Partnerships](12-partnerships.md)

## What makes the idea different?
The emphasis is coherence: connecting physical work to its requirements, evidence and manufacturing knowledge, so intelligence has relevant context. That is the intended approach, not proof of superiority over another platform. [Ontology](05-ontology.md)

## Will AI run the factory on its own?
The public principle is “AI investigates. Evidence supports. Humans decide.” Intelligence is intended to support work within appropriate human and system responsibilities, not to replace machine-control safeguards. [Intelligence](06-intelligence.md)

## Can I see the source code or internal architecture?
I can explain the public roles of execution, context and intelligence. Proprietary implementation details are outside this guide. For an integration discussion, we can start with the outcome and interface needs you want to explore. [VOS](04-vos.md)

## Are you competing with global industrial technology companies?
Global competitiveness in selected areas is a long-term ambition. This knowledge base does not establish feature parity, certifications or comparative benchmarks. [Roadmap](03-roadmap.md)

## How do people fit into the vision?
Individual excellence and shared purpose belong together. Voltron’s culture direction values depth, craft, courage, imagination and humanity alongside technical capability. [Human Lattice](10-human-lattice.md)

## Are Commons fellowships open?
Commons is a developing vision. I do not have confirmation of an open fellowship or application deadline. A relevant collaboration can be discussed through a verified contact route. [Commons](11-commons.md)

## What are the prices, certifications and capacity?
This package contains no verified figures or certificates. Those details must be confirmed for the relevant product or production program. [Claim register](../maintenance/claims.md)

## Have you saved my inquiry?
Only a successful response from the connected submission service can confirm that. Otherwise, I can help prepare a summary for you to send. [Inquiry behavior](../agent/enquiries.md)
