# Ten-year direction and milestones

## Status
An indicative vision, not a delivery commitment. Years are measured from the start of operations, as described on the current website. No calendar start date is established here. Do not convert relative years into dates or represent milestones as completed.

| Phase | Intended direction | Public technology description | Evidence needed before claiming completion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Years 1–2 | Develop and prove Alpha | Connect execution, manufacturing context, quality evidence and intelligence in the initial surface-treatment environment. | Verified operating readiness and documented validation. |
| Years 3–4 | Expand demonstrated capability | Carry reviewed process knowledge and configurations into selected additional operating environments or partnerships. | Deployment-specific readiness, repeatability and accountable support. |
| Year 5 | Begin dedicated product development alongside expansion | Select recurring industrial problems for focused product discovery, engineering and validation. | Approved product program, scoped problem and validation plan. |
| Years 6–7 | Compete globally | Selected products pursue markets with dependable quality and support. | Customer validation, product quality, support and market readiness. |
| Years 8–10 | Establish the brand | Original products, sustained quality and lifecycle support earn lasting trust. | Repeat demand, original capability and dependable lifecycle support. |

## How growth connects
Operating experience informs reusable capability. Reusable capability can support expansion. Recurring needs can inform proprietary product opportunities. Each step still requires engineering, validation and judgment; expansion is not automatic replication.

## What is not promised
No fixed factory count, revenue, valuation, market share, product catalog or launch date is established here. Dedicated product development is a future direction, not proof that products can be ordered now. “Exponential growth” expresses ambition, not a forecast.
