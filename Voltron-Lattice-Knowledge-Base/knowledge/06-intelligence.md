# Industrial intelligence and human judgment

## Short answer
Voltron’s intelligence direction is contextual assistance for industrial work: help people understand evidence, investigate questions and prepare responsible decisions.

## Intended assistance
| Need | Public explanation |
|---|---|
| Understand a situation | Bring the relevant manufacturing context together. |
| Find knowledge | Locate applicable documents or evidence within permitted access. |
| Investigate a deviation | Help compare observations and identify questions to examine. |
| Prepare a decision | Organize the reasoning, evidence and remaining uncertainty. |
| Learn from experience | Carry reviewed lessons into reusable knowledge. |

These are intended roles, not a list of certified, deployed features. The website guide explains Voltron; it cannot inspect a visitor’s factory or diagnose production without an authorized, separately implemented system.

## Automation and autonomy
Automation can repeat a defined operation. Broader autonomy requires reliable context, constraints, uncertainty handling and clear responsibility. Voltron’s public story should explain why these conditions matter without claiming that autonomous operation has already been achieved.

## Working with people
People remain responsible for direction, judgment and consequential decisions. Useful AI should make evidence and uncertainty easier to examine, not hide them behind fluent language. No guaranteed productivity, defect reduction or cost-saving percentage is available in this corpus.
